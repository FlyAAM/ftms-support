# Privacy Policy for SOULFAM RACE

**Last updated: June 19, 2026**

**SOULFAM RACE** is developed and operated by **Anatoliy Moskalenko**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the SOULFAM RACE Android application collects or processes, why it is used, where it may be stored, and what choices users have.

## 1. Information processed by the application

### Bluetooth fitness equipment

The application scans for and connects to Bluetooth Low Energy fitness equipment that supports FTMS or compatible protocols.

The application may process:

- Bluetooth device name and hardware address;
- equipment type and connection status;
- equipment capabilities;
- workout data transmitted by the equipment.

On Android 11 and earlier, Android may require location permission to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. SOULFAM RACE uses this permission only for Bluetooth device discovery. The application does not request, collect, store, or transmit the user’s GPS coordinates.

### Workout and fitness data

Depending on the connected equipment, workout data may include:

- distance;
- elapsed time;
- speed;
- pace;
- cadence;
- power;
- resistance or incline;
- stroke count;
- calories;
- heart rate, when supplied by the equipment.

Workout data is used to display the current workout, calculate progress, control supported equipment, run workout programs, and calculate race results.

Most personal workout settings and locally created workout programs are stored on the user’s device using Android application storage.

### Online profile

To use online races, a user creates an online racing profile. The profile may contain:

- display name;
- country or “International” designation;
- public Online ID;
- private list of favorite users and user-defined aliases;
- Firebase anonymous authentication identifier;
- profile creation, update, and last activity timestamps.

The display name, country, and public Online ID are intended to be visible to other authenticated SOULFAM RACE users.

The application uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It does not require the user to provide an email address, phone number, password, or Google account to participate in online races.

### Online races

Online race information may include:

- race title, settings, status, and creator;
- participant names, countries, Online IDs, readiness status, and team information;
- live distance, speed, progress, and update timestamps;
- finishing order, finishing time, and race results;
- invitations and favorite-user relationships required by online race features.

Authenticated users may be able to view available races, participant lists, and race results, including results of races in which they did not participate. Private race visibility and invitations may restrict access to joining a race, but should not be treated as a mechanism for storing confidential information.

Users should not enter sensitive personal information in display names, race names, aliases, or other public text fields.

### Application identifiers and access status

The application generates an installation identifier and uses Firebase identifiers to:

- maintain application access and trial status;
- synchronize online profiles and races;
- diagnose support requests;
- prevent accidental identity conflicts;
- apply remote feature configuration.

These identifiers are not used for advertising.

### Analytics

The Google Firebase Analytics SDK is included in the application. It may process application interaction, device, application version, approximate technical, and diagnostic information according to Google Firebase terms and settings.

Analytics is used to understand application stability and feature usage. SOULFAM RACE does not use this information to provide personalized advertising.

The application does not currently include Firebase Crashlytics.

## 2. How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

- discover and connect to compatible fitness equipment;
- display and calculate workout metrics;
- operate workout programs and local races;
- create profiles and provide online multiplayer races;
- synchronize participants and race progress;
- display participant lists and race results;
- manage feature access, trial status, and remote configuration;
- maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems;
- respond to support and privacy requests.

## 3. Service providers and data sharing

SOULFAM RACE does not sell personal data.

Online and analytics features use Google Firebase services, including:

- Firebase Authentication;
- Cloud Firestore;
- Firebase Realtime Database;
- Firebase Remote Config;
- Firebase Analytics.

Google acts as a service provider and may process technical and service data according to its own terms and privacy documentation:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the application, or the developer from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Personal data is not shared with third parties for targeted advertising.

## 4. Data storage and retention

### Data stored on the device

Saved equipment, preferences, workout programs, local race presets, and other local application data remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears the application’s storage, or uninstalls the application.

### Online profiles

Online profile data is retained while the profile is used or until deletion is requested. Some minimal records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or resolving an active support request.

### Online races

Finished, canceled, and inactive race records are intended to be temporary. The application attempts to remove finished and canceled races after approximately 24 hours and stale active races after a shorter operational period.

Because cleanup may depend on application or service activity, deletion may occur later than the stated period. Users must not rely on online races as permanent storage.

### Analytics and service logs

Firebase analytics, authentication, and operational service data is retained according to the applicable Firebase configuration, Google’s retention controls, and legitimate security or operational needs.

## 5. Security

Data sent to Firebase services is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

Access to online data is restricted using Firebase Authentication and database security rules. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. User choices and controls

Users can:

- use local features without creating an online racing profile;
- stop participating in an online race;
- edit their online display name and country;
- delete locally saved trainers and locally stored application data;
- clear application storage or uninstall the application;
- request deletion of their online profile and associated online data.

## 8. Children’s privacy

SOULFAM RACE is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required to consent to data processing in the user’s country. The application should be used by minors only with the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so that the information can be reviewed and deleted.

## 9. International processing

Firebase services may process data on servers located outside the user’s country. Such processing is subject to Google’s applicable data protection terms and safeguards.
