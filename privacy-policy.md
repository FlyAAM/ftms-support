# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

##Overview

r.crew respects your privacy.

This application is designed to work with Bluetooth FTMS-compatible fitness equipment and online multiplayer races.

## Data Collection

The application may collect and temporarily process:

- Bluetooth device information
- workout metrics
- online race data
- race results
- device identifiers required for synchronization
- application diagnostics and crash information

Workout metrics may include:
- distance
- speed
- cadence
-power
- pace
- calories
- heart rate (if supported by device)

## Online Features

Online races use Firebase services for synchronization between participants.

Race information and temporary race history may be stored online for multiplayer functionality.

Finished and canceled races are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

## Bluetooth Permissions

The application uses Bluetooth permissions to connect to FTMS-compatible fitness equipment.

Bluetooth access is required for:
- device discovery
- trainer connection
- workout data synchronization

## Internet Access

Internet access is used for:
- online races
- multiplayer synchronization
- temporary race storage
- Firebase services

##Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal data.

Data is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Security

Online data is transmitted using secure encrypted network connections (HTTPS/TLS).

## User Control

Users may stop using online functionality at any time by disconnecting from online races.

Temporary race data is automatically removed after the retention period.
